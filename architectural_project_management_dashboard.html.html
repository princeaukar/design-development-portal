Project Initiation & Feasibility Analysis

1. Project Initiation: Starts as an Idea

Every great commercial or residential landmark begins as a conceptual spark. During initiation, the owner defines the core vision, spatial requirements, and high-level objectives that guide the entire lifecycle.

2. Feasibility Evaluation & Go/No-Go Gate

Before committing substantial capital, a rigorous feasibility study evaluates four critical pillars:

Cost of Money

Interest rates, loan terms, and capital expenditure forecasting.

Schedule

Timeline feasibility from design through groundbreaking and handover.

Budget

Total estimated construction cost vs owner equity and debt capacity.

Market Demand

Tenant absorption rates, commercial competition, and ROI projection.

Go / No-Go Decision

Executive review has validated all feasibility parameters for Apex Horizon.
